Prompt Seedance (Gratis)

Copy → Dreamina → Generate
P1 Hero — Jaringan Data Untuk: hero section
Cinematic macro shot of a golden neural network gradually illuminating in deep black space. Light travels along thin connecting lines from the center outward, particles drift slowly. Slow camera push-in, shallow depth of field, warm golden bokeh in background. Polished, premium, 16:9, 5 seconds, no text, no watermark, no logos.
P2 MINA — Terminal Berjalan Untuk: video narasi MINA
Cinematic close-up of a small glowing terminal window on a dark desk, command lines appearing one by one as if being typed. Warm screen glow reflects on the surface. Slow dolly movement to the right, shallow depth of field, moody lighting. Professional tech aesthetic, 16:9, 5 seconds, no readable text, no watermark, no logos.
P3 Gated Portal — Gerbang Terbuka Untuk: video narasi Gated Portal
Cinematic shot of a minimalist dark door with warm orange light gradually appearing through the edges as it opens slightly. Abstract geometric frame around the door, particles of light floating. Slow camera orbit, premium architectural aesthetic, 16:9, 5 seconds, no text, no watermark, no logos.
P4 Spareparts — Rak Bergerak Untuk: video narasi Spareparts
Cinematic shot of organized dark storage shelves, warm accent lights turning on sequentially row by row. Clean industrial aesthetic, slow camera tilt upward. Minimal, premium, 16:9, 5 seconds, no readable text, no watermark, no logos.
💡

Semua prompt ini gratis — pakai kredit harian Dreamina (Seedance 2.0 / Lite). Setiap frasa "no text, no watermark, no logos" penting supaya hasilnya bersih tanpa perlu dihapus manual seperti video hero sebelumnya.

05

Cara Pakai

Impor desain ke Figma

Buka Figma → drag file design/figma-import/*.svg ke canvas. Figma otomatis mengenali layer, teks, dan warna — semuanya bisa diedit.

Generate video gratis

Buka dreamina.capcut.com → AI Video → pilih Seedance 2.0 → paste salah satu prompt di atas → Generate. Kredit harian gratis cukup untuk beberapa klip.

Bersihkan & optimalkan

Kalau ada watermark tersisa, kirim ke saya — saya hapus dengan teknik delogo yang sama seperti video hero (terverifikasi 122/122 frame bersih).

Integrasi ke portofolio

Kirim file video — saya pasang di halaman proyek dengan pemutar yang menghormati prefers-reduced-motion, lazy-load, dan format WebM + MP4.
